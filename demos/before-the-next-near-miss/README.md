# Before the Next Near Miss

An independent, eight-page awareness course by Patrick Ordish. 20–25 minutes including practice. New course design and content wrap three unchanged, previously generated Synthesia videos: Hazard, Risk and Control (55s); Controls That Reduce Risk (75s); Learning from a Near Miss (50s). No new videos were generated or edited for this course.

## Course experience

Eight optional synthetic UK English page readers and exact transcripts; three existing videos with original spoken transcripts; eight original labelled diagrams; fact/assumption tabs; three practice decisions; six retryable challenge questions; a generic next-shift reflection; downloadable pocket checklist and text plan. Video pages can be completed using the transcript. A native version is also published in Patrick Ordish's Nucleus LMS provider account.

## Delivery

Serve index.html over HTTP. No dependencies or build step. The downloadable SCORM 1.2 package launches the same course as one SCO. It records page, compact progress/answers, a 0–100 practice score, session time and completion. Completion requires all eight pages visited, all six challenge questions answered and the Complete course button. No pass threshold. Free writing is never placed in LMS suspend data. Outside an LMS, practice progress stays in this browser; writing stays in the open page and can be downloaded on request.

## Scope and sources

The fictional Bay 4 exercise supports awareness and discussion. It does not replace site risk assessment, operating instructions, competent supervision or equipment authorisation. It does not establish practical competence or award an accredited qualification.

HSE: [Separating pedestrians and vehicles](https://www.hse.gov.uk/workplacetransport/separating.htm) and [Risk assessment](https://www.hse.gov.uk/workplacetransport/management/risk.htm), checked 15 November 2026. New scenario and questions are original. Existing video scripts retain their source wording.
