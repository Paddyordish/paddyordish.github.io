BEFORE THE NEXT NEAR MISS — POCKET CHECKLIST
Patrick Ordish | Independent awareness course

NOTICE
What could cause harm? Who might be exposed? Keep facts separate from assumptions.

CONTROL
Follow current site instructions, routes and authorised controls. If unsafe, keep clear and use the escalation route. Do not improvise changes or operate beyond your authorisation.

REPORT
Use the site process: what happened, where, when, immediate action and uncertainty. Ask who owns follow-up and how effectiveness will be checked.
