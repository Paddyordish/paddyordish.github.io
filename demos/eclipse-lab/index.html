Make the Sun disappear.

One Moon. One Sun. Can you line them up to make a total solar eclipse?

A quick classroom investigation

Learning goal: explain that a solar eclipse happens when the Moon moves between Earth and the Sun, blocking our view of the Sun.

Explore: move the Moon across the Sun and collect a partial and total eclipse. Explain: ask pairs which objects line up and why the Sun still shines when it disappears from view.

This activity shows the apparent discs of the Sun and Moon from one observer on Earth. Sizes, movement and timing are simplified. The changing sky is an illustration, not a brightness measurement.
